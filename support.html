Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored exclusively on your iPhone, inside Balio's local sandbox. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and we have no access to your records. Deleting the app permanently removes all stored data.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Balio is account-free by design. Open the app and start tracking — there's nothing to sign up for.

How do I back up my data?

You can export your full data set to CSV or a text summary at any time from Settings → Export. Save the file to iCloud Drive, Files, or another backup location of your choice. Standard iOS device backups (iTunes / iCloud backup) will also include Balio's local data if those backups are enabled on your device.

How do I transfer my data to a new iPhone?

When restoring from an iCloud or iTunes backup on the same device, Balio's local data will come along automatically. If you move to a new device without restoring a backup, use Settings → Export to save a CSV before the transfer and re-import on the new device.

Is Balio a financial advisor?

No. Balio is a personal journaling and tracking tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Any numbers and charts produced by the app are based solely on the data you enter. Always consult a qualified professional for advice tailored to your situation.

What asset classes can I track?

Balio supports stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, real estate, retirement accounts, cash equivalents, commodities, and digital assets. Each holding records quantity, average cost, current price, account, purchase date, and notes — with automatic market value, unrealized gain/loss, and return percentage.

How do I attach a receipt to a transaction?

Open the transaction and tap the receipt / attachment control. You can capture a new image using the camera or pick an existing one from your photo library. Receipts are stored locally on your device, with pinch-to-zoom fullscreen inspection.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data and confirm. Alternatively, deleting the app from your device permanently and irreversibly removes every record, receipt, budget, and goal.

Does Balio support iPad or Mac?

Balio is built for iPhone. iPad support depends on device compatibility. For the latest list of supported devices, see the App Store listing.
