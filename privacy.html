Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Short version: Balio is built on a single principle — your financial data is yours alone. We don't collect it, we don't sync it to the cloud, and we don't share it with anyone. Every transaction, holding, receipt, and goal you record stays on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Balio ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. By using Balio, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. Balio is designed to operate without servers, accounts, or remote storage. Specifically:

  • No account registration — Balio does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address to use the app.
  • No analytics or telemetry — We do not use any third-party analytics, SDKs, or tracking frameworks.
  • No advertising identifiers — Balio does not collect IDFA, GAID, or any device advertising identifier.
  • No usage data — We do not record which features you use, how often you open the app, or any behavioral patterns.

2.1 Data you store locally

The App stores the following information on your device only, encrypted by the operating system and accessible solely to you:

  • Transactions (amounts, dates, categories, accounts, notes)
  • Holdings (asset names, quantities, costs, prices, accounts, notes)
  • Receipts (images captured via camera or imported from your photo library)
  • Budgets (monthly limits and category rules)
  • Goals (targets, milestones, contributions, notes)
  • App preferences (currency, theme, etc.)

3. Permissions We Request

Balio requests only the permissions strictly necessary for its core functionality. Each permission is optional and you can deny it without losing access to other features.

Permission Purpose Data destination
Camera Capture receipt images to attach to a transaction or record. Stored locally only
Photo Library (read) Attach existing receipt or document images to a record. Stored locally only
Photo Library (write) Save exported financial reports or charts you choose to export. Saved to your photo library

Balio never uploads images, transactions, or any other content from your device to a remote server. All image processing happens on-device.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data you enter into Balio is stored exclusively in the app's local sandbox on your iPhone. The data is protected by iOS sandboxing and may additionally be encrypted through device-level protections such as the device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

Because Balio does not transmit your data to any server, there is no remote copy of your financial records. Deleting the app from your device permanently and irreversibly removes all stored data.

5. Data Exports

If you choose to export your data, Balio generates a CSV file or text summary and saves it to a location you select (such as the Files app or your photo library). Those exports are governed by the storage destinations you choose — Balio does not access, scan, or transmit exported files once they are saved.

6. Children

Balio is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no information at all, this policy applies uniformly to all users.

7. Third-Party Services

Balio contains no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or social integrations. There are no third parties with access to your data through the App.

8. International Users

Because Balio does not transfer your data to servers located in any country, cross-border data transfer regulations such as the GDPR (EU), UK GDPR, CCPA (California), LGPD (Brazil), or PIPL (China) do not require disclosures about international transfers from us. Your data does not leave your device.

9. Your Rights

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. You have full and exclusive control over the data on your device. You may:

  • View, edit, or delete any record inside the App at any time.
  • Export your full data set to CSV at any time.
  • Permanently erase all data by deleting the app from your device.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Because Balio does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Disclaimer

Balio is a personal journaling and tracking tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Any numbers, totals, or visualizations produced by the App are based solely on the data you enter and should not be relied upon as the basis for investment decisions. Always consult a qualified financial professional for advice tailored to your situation.
